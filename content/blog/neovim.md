## Why use neovim

"VSCode exists, so why bother with something as archaic as Vim?" I used to wonder the same thing until I gave Neovim a try—and I’ve never looked back.

At first, transitioning from the user-friendly interface of VSCode to the command-based environment of Vim can seem daunting. Commands like :q! for <cite> quitting [^1] </cite> might feel cryptic at first, but it quickly becomes intuitive once you get the hang of it.

[^1]: Infamous stackoverlow [post](https://stackoverflow.com/questions/11828270/how-do-i-exit-vim) 

I highly recommend trying the Vim plugin in VSCode (or your editor of choice) to familiarize yourself with the Vim bindings. It’s a great way to ease into the experience and discover the power and efficiency Vim offers.
